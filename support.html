Common questions

  • I bought a skin but it isn't unlocked. Open Skins → Packs → Restore purchases. It asks the App Store for everything bought with your Apple ID.
  • How do I play? Press anywhere, pull back a little, then flick up toward the holes. A short flick rolls the ball, a longer swipe lobs it. You can replay the guide from the menu with How to play.
  • Who am I playing against? A live player near your rating, or a practice opponent when nobody is online. The match screen tells you which.
  • How do I report a player or a name? In Ranks → Friends, tap ··· next to the player, then Report or Block.
  • How do I delete my data? In Settings (or Ranks → Friends), tap Delete my data. Purchases stay with your Apple ID.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
