Support

Real Hole Toss · last updated 26 October 2026

Real Hole Toss is a 1v1 ball-toss game for iPhone and iPad. If you need help or want to report a problem, we are happy to hear from you.

Contact

Email us at nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com. We usually reply within a couple of days. Please tell us your device model and iOS version so we can help faster.

Common questions

  • Restore my purchases. Open the game, go to Skins → Packs → Restore purchases. Skins you bought are tied to your Apple ID and come back on any device.
  • Turn score sharing on or off. Open Settings in the game. You can switch the global leaderboard on or off at any time; with it off, the game works fully offline and nothing is sent.
  • Delete my data. In Settings (or Ranks → Friends) choose Delete my data. This permanently removes your name, rating, friends and match history.
  • A player's name or behaviour. You can report or block any player from their profile in the Friends list.
