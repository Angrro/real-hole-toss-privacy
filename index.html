Privacy Policy

Hole Toss Original · last updated 26 October 2026

Hole Toss Original is a 1v1 ball-toss game for iPhone and iPad. This page explains what the game stores about you, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Nicolae Florin Alexandru, an independent developer based in Romania. Contact: nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com.

Playing without sharing anything

On first launch the game asks whether you want to share your scores on the online leaderboard. If you choose Not now, you play offline against practice opponents, your progress stays on your device and nothing is sent to our server. You can change this choice at any time in Settings.

What we never do

  • No ads, no analytics tools and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for marketing.
  • Best-throw clips are made on your phone and shared only where you choose. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Push notifications are only about your matches and invites, never advertising.

Who else handles the data

  • Supabase hosts our database and server in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase acts only on our instructions under a data processing agreement. If Supabase staff ever access data from outside the EU, it is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Apple processes payments and delivers push notifications, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Every provider that handles user data for us is bound to give it the same or equal protection as described in this policy. We don't share data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile, rating, friends, matches, throws and purchases: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all of it is deleted with it.
  • Reports you send stay after you delete your account, without your name attached, so a reported player can still be reviewed.
  • Hosting logs: as long as our hosting provider keeps them, normally a few days.

Withdrawing consent and deleting your data

In Settings, turn off Share my scores on the global leaderboard to stop online play, or tap Delete my data to permanently delete your account, name, rating, friends and match history. You can also write to us and we delete it within 30 days. Skins you bought stay with your Apple ID and come back with Restore purchases.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, restrict or object to how it is used, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to the address above and we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or the authority in your country.

Automated decisions

The server checks scores automatically and may flag a suspicious score for a person to review. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you by automated means alone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect more than described above from anyone. Player names are filtered for offensive words, and every player can be reported or blocked. If you believe a child under 13 has turned on online play, write to us and we will delete the account.
