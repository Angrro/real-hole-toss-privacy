Privacy Policy

Real Hole Toss · last updated 15 October 2026

Real Hole Toss is a 1v1 ball-toss game for iPhone. This page explains what the game stores about you and why. We keep it to what the game needs to work.

Who we are

Real Hole Toss is made by Flo, an independent developer based in Romania. Contact: nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com.

What we store

  • An account ID. The app creates an account for you automatically the first time you open it. You don't give us an email or a password.
  • Your player name and country tag (for example Flo#RO). Other players see your name, country, rating and the skins you use.
  • Game data: your rating, matches played and won, win streak, friends list, players you blocked, and the throws you made in each match. The server uses your throws to check every score, so the leaderboard stays fair.
  • Purchases: which skins and packs you bought and the App Store transaction ID, so we can unlock them and restore them. Payment is handled by Apple. We never see your card or Apple ID.
  • A push notification token, only if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when someone challenges you.
  • Reports you send about another player's name or behaviour.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • Clips of your best throws are made on your phone and shared only where you choose. They are never uploaded to us.

Where it is kept

Game data is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Purchases are verified with Apple's App Store Server API.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Matches older than two years may be removed.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Ranks → Friends → Delete my account. This permanently removes your name, rating, friends and match history. Skins you bought stay with your Apple ID and come back with Restore purchases on a new account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Write to the address above and we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP).

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect more data from children than described above. Player names are filtered for offensive words, and any player can be reported or blocked.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
